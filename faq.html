Installation steps, commands, and answers for your Minecraft server.

Quickstart Setup (60 Seconds)

  1. Download the Icelite Anticheat jar provided with your license or release package.
  2. Drop the .jar into your /plugins folder.
  3. Restart your server.
  4. Enter your license key in plugins/Icelite/config.yml.
  5. Run /icelite reload in console.
# plugins/Icelite/config.yml license-key: "YOUR-LICENSE-KEY" webhook: enabled: true discord-url: "https://discord.com/api/webhooks/..." checks: combat: reach: max-distance: 3.00 action: "CANCEL_ATTACK"
