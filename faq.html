Quickstart Setup (60 Seconds)

  1. Download the Icelite Anticheat .jar file provided with your license.
  2. Place the .jar into your server's /plugins directory.
  3. Restart your Minecraft server or execute your server start script.
  4. Open plugins/Icelite/config.yml and paste your authorization key.
  5. Run /icelite reload in console or in-game.
