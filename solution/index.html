Native packet interception paired with deterministic physics simulation. Zero lag, zero false bans.

1. Low-Level Netty Packet Interception

Icelite hooks directly into Netty's channel pipeline before packets reach the Minecraft main tick loop. Illegal combat packets are cancelled instantly in sub-millisecond time.

[Netty Channel] → [Icelite Byte Interceptor] → [Instant Cancel/Filter] → [Vanilla Tick Loop]

Evaluated asynchronously across dedicated worker threads with zero main-thread TPS drop.

2. Deterministic Vanilla Physics Engine

Simulates the exact vanilla Minecraft movement formulas to eliminate false flags:

  • Ground friction calculation based on block type (Ice, Slime, Soul Sand).
  • Inertia decay and gravity acceleration tracking per player tick.
  • Precise bounding-box raycasting against player position history buffers.

Combat checks compute exact mathematical line-of-sight to prevent bypasses without hindering legitimate PvP mechanics.

3. Cloud Heuristics & Replay Profiling

[Client Actions] → [Tick Ring Buffer] → [Cloud Heuristic Model] → [Deterministic Verdict]

Suspicious patterns are evaluated across thousands of ticks. Sudden bursts, rotation irregularities, and click jitter are cross-referenced with millisecond accuracy.
