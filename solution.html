Architecture

The Icelite Solution

Native packet interception paired with cloud-offloaded heuristics. Zero lag, zero false bans.

1. Low-Level Netty Packet Interceptor

Icelite hooks directly into Netty's channel pipeline before packets reach the Minecraft main tick loop. Illegal combat packets are cancelled instantly in sub-millisecond time.

[Netty Channel] → [Icelite Byte Interceptor] → [Instant Cancel/Filter] → [Vanilla Tick Loop]

Evaluated asynchronously across dedicated worker threads with zero main-thread TPS drop.

2. Deterministic Vanilla Physics Engine

Simulates the exact vanilla Minecraft movement formulas to eliminate false flags:

  • Ground friction calculation based on block type (Ice, Slime, Soul Sand).
  • Inertia decay and gravity acceleration tracking.
  • Knockback velocity vector decay verification.

3. Cloud Edge Telemetry & 3D Replay

Heavy statistical analysis (mouse curves, autoclicker kurtosis) is offloaded to Icelite Cloud. Whenever a violation occurs, 3D coordinate packets are saved to your dashboard for instant staff replay review.
